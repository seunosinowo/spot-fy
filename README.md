# Soundhalla

Soundhalla is a playlist promotion platform that helps independent creators get their music placed on major streaming platforms. Submit once, and we pitch your tracks to curated playlists across Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Audiomack and Tunenova.

## What we do

- **Playlist placement** - Get your music in front of real listeners through curated playlists.
- **Algorithmic growth** - Playlist adds send strong signals that unlock editorial recommendations and algorithmic discovery.
- **Monetization** - Turn playlist exposure into royalties, sync opportunities and paid communities.
- **Creator support** - Get hands-on help from a team that understands independent artists.

## Platforms

| Platform | What you get |
|----------|-------------|
| Spotify | Curated playlist placement and Discover Weekly growth |
| Apple Music | Editorial and independent playlist placement |
| YouTube | Shorts, music videos and creator channel sync |
| Audiomack | African and global playlist promotion |
| Tunenova | Targeted playlist and fan campaigns |

## How it works

1. **Upload your track** - Drop your audio, artwork and release info.
2. **Pick your platforms** - Choose where you want playlist placement.
3. **Get reviewed** - Our curators and A&R team review every submission.
4. **Grow & get paid** - Watch streams climb and withdraw earnings to your bank.
